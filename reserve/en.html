About Wu Hill

One tea garden, three rooms, just the right distance.

Wu Hill sits deep in a tea garden on Xiding, Alishan — a small tea house with only three guest rooms. We chose not to fill the building with rooms, so every window keeps enough mountain, cloud and light. Open the curtains at dawn and the tea bushes are still wet with dew; after dark, the valley is quiet enough to hear yourself breathe.

Booking Policy

Reservations are open from tomorrow up to 3 months ahead. Same-day bookings are not accepted.

Rate Tiers

Rates fall into three tiers — weekday, weekend, and peak/consecutive holidays — shown live on the calendar. Dates already booked appear struck through with no price.

Bookings cannot be cancelled, refunded, or changed from the day before check-in onward. Please double-check your dates before submitting.
