About WU HILL

An old tea factory,
quietly awakened among the tea fields.

WU HILL was once a small tea factory in Xiding.
Tea leaves were withered, rolled, and roasted here,
and life in the mountains moved with every harvest.

Eventually, the factory fell silent.
We kept its original timber beams, stone walls, and the intimate proportions of the old building,
gently transforming it into a retreat with just three rooms.

There is no grand lobby, and no itinerary waiting to fill your day.

What we wanted to preserve was what the mountain had always offered:

mist, light, the scent of tea, and the feeling of time slowing down.

Nearby Attractions

ALISHAN NEARBY

When you feel like venturing out, some of Alishan’s most beautiful places are just a short drive away.
Tea field trails, mountain-town streets and the forest railway are all within an hour.

Eryanping Trail

Tea fields, sunsets and seas of clouds

The trail climbs along a tea-covered ridge, and late afternoon is the finest hour. On clear days you can watch the clouds rise from the valley below.

Approx. 8 minutes by car

Shizhuo Trails

Mist Trail, Tea Trail and cedar forests

Five trails, each with its own character: the Mist Trail winds through cedar and fog, the Tea Trail crosses open tea fields. One trail fills a half day nicely.

Approx. 15 minutes by car

Fenqihu

Old streets, forest railway and a mountain-town lunch

A mountain town of old streets and a historic station, well suited to a long lunch. Crowds gather around midday, so arriving early keeps it quiet.

Approx. 25 minutes by car

Alishan National Forest Recreation Area

Forest railway, ancient trees and Zhushan sunrise

If Zhushan sunrise is the plan, turn in early the night before. The park’s paths are gentle, ideal for a slow morning walk.

Approx. 60 minutes by car

FAQ

Your first stay at WU HILL?

Will I definitely see the sunrise or sea of clouds? ＋

Sunrise, clouds and stars all depend on the weather, so unfortunately, we cannot guarantee them.
Perhaps that is part of what makes the mountains beautiful—no two days ever look quite the same.

Can I visit without a car? ＋

Yes. Public transportation is available to stops near Xiding.
However, if you plan to explore the surrounding area, driving is the most convenient option.

Is tea included with my stay? ＋

Yes. Every overnight guest is invited to enjoy our welcome tea.
No knowledge of tea ceremony is required.

Can children stay at WU HILL? ＋

Yes. Children are welcome in selected room types.
Please refer to each room description for occupancy limits.

How do I get here? ＋

No. 58-7, Xiding, Gongtian Village, Fanlu Township, Chiayi County 602, Taiwan. Driving from Chiayi city via Provincial Highway 18 takes about 50 minutes.
You can also travel to Chiayi Station by HSR or rail, then take the Alishan bus line and get off at Xiding.
